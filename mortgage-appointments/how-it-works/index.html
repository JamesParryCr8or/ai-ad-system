A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Clear answers.
Before you commit.

What exactly counts as a show?

A prospect who meets the qualification criteria agreed for your campaign and attends the scheduled consultation. Before launch, we’ll document how attendance is verified and how late arrivals, duplicates, cancellations and disputed appointments are handled.

How much does it cost?

We quote based on your target cases, coverage and campaign setup. Your proposal will separate the show fee from any ad spend, setup or other costs. Submitting this form or booking a discovery call is free and doesn’t commit you to buy.

What if a prospect doesn’t turn up?

There is no appointment fee for a no-show. If they reschedule, attendance is assessed at the new appointment. We’ll agree the reporting process and any credit arrangements before launch.

Are these mortgage approvals or guaranteed clients?

No. Qualification establishes whether an enquiry meets the agreed marketing criteria. Your advisers remain responsible for fact-finding, advice and assessing the client’s circumstances. Attendance does not guarantee an application, approval, completion or revenue.

Can you work with an appointed representative?

We can discuss your setup and your network’s requirements. Any campaign would need the appropriate permissions and your firm or principal’s approval before going live. If you’re still setting up, we can discuss readiness without promising an immediate launch.

Is this a shared lead marketplace?

This is a campaign service built around your brokerage. On the call we’ll explain where enquiries come from, how they are routed and the allocation terms for your campaign.

How soon can we start?

After we’ve agreed the offer, criteria, costs and delivery setup, we prepare the campaign for your approval. Launch timing depends on the assets, integrations and approvals needed. We don’t promise a fixed appointment volume or start date before scoping the work.
