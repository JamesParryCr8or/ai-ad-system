No. Every generation and render runs in the cloud via CR8OR AI Studio. You don't need a powerful GPU, and you don't need to be a Premiere or After Effects expert. The manual teaches you how to direct the output compositionally.

How is this different from buying Midjourney or Runway separately?

Buying individual subscriptions costs over $160/month, and none of those tools share client memory. CR8OR gives you 12 video engines and 11 image engines in one dashboard with zero monthly subscriptions—you only pay for what you render via usage billing.

Do I ever have to show my face or record my voice?

Never. You are building commercial, product, and review video assets for brands using AI avatars, licensed voices, and cinematic 3D/video engines. You operate completely behind the scenes.

How quickly can I land my first $500–$1,000 client?

The 30-Day sprint in Chapter 13 shows you how to build a 3-brand portfolio in week 2 and deploy 10 spec outreach emails a day in week 3. Most disciplined students secure their initial paid project within their first 21–30 days.
