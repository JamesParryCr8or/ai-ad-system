Guarantee terms and disclaimer

  1. What is being guaranteed. This guarantee is a refund of the £27 pack fee. It is not a guarantee of sales, revenue, profit or any particular level of performance. Advertising results depend on your product, offer, pricing, website, targeting, budget and market.
  2. How ROAS is measured. Return on ad spend is taken from Meta Ads Manager as purchase conversion value divided by amount spent, using the attribution setting already configured on your account at the point the benchmark is agreed. We use your account’s reported figures, not a third-party analytics tool.
  3. The benchmark. Your benchmark is the highest-ROAS static image ad in your account during the comparison period, with at least £20 of spend against it. It must be agreed in writing with us before your new creatives go live. If no benchmark is agreed in advance, there is nothing to measure against and the guarantee does not apply.
  4. The comparison period. Performance is compared against the immediately preceding period of equal length, week on week. A seven-day test is compared with the seven days before it.
  5. Minimum spend. Each creative included in a claim must have at least £20 of ad spend recorded against it. Creatives below that threshold are excluded from the assessment.
  6. Account verification. We must be granted read-only access to the Meta ad account before the test period begins, and it must remain in place while a claim is assessed. Claims on accounts we cannot verify cannot be processed.
  7. Conditions must stay stable. Your product, pricing, offer, landing page and conversion tracking must remain materially unchanged for the duration of the test. Changing the offer or the destination page mid-test invalidates the comparison.
  8. Where a claim cannot be assessed. We cannot assess claims where conversion tracking is broken or misconfigured, where the pixel or Conversions API is not reporting, where the ad account is restricted or suspended, where data has been deleted, or where delivered creatives have been materially altered before running.
  9. Claim window. Claims must be raised within 30 days of delivery of your pack. One claim per customer and per ad account.
  10. Ad spend is never refunded. The guarantee covers the CR8OR AI pack fee only. Money paid to Meta or any other advertising platform, and any third-party costs, sit outside this guarantee and are not recoverable from us.
  11. Results shown on this page. Screenshots and case studies elsewhere on this page are examples of work delivered for other clients. They are illustrations of the approach, not projections of what your account will produce.

To raise a claim, or to agree your benchmark before launching, contact us and we will walk you through it.

Questions Before You Start

What types of businesses is this for?

It is designed primarily for ecommerce brands with a physical or clearly visual product.

Are all 40 ads completely different?

They are built from proven format families such as comparison, proof, urgency, education and native-style creative. Some will share the same offer or product imagery, but each is designed around a different hook, layout or testing angle.

Do you run the advertisements for me?

Campaign setup is not included in the £27 pack, but can be added after checkout.

What do I need to provide?

Your product URL, logo, brand colours, product imagery and any claims or restrictions we must observe.

Can you guarantee sales?

No, and we would not trust anyone who did. Sales depend on your product, offer, website, targeting, budget and market. What we do guarantee is the creative: if none of the 40 ads beats your best performing static ad on ROAS under the conditions set out in our guarantee terms, you get the full £27 back.

How does the ROAS guarantee actually work?

Before you launch, you tell us your best performing static ad and its ROAS, and we agree that figure as your benchmark. You run the new creatives with at least £20 of spend on each one, and we compare the test period against the prior period, week on week, using your own Meta Ads Manager figures. If nothing in the pack beats the benchmark, we refund the pack fee in full.

Why do you need access to my ad account?

So both sides are measuring the same thing. Read-only access lets us agree your benchmark up front and verify a claim afterwards from the account’s own reporting, rather than relying on screenshots. We do not need permission to spend, edit campaigns or post on your behalf.

Why the £20 minimum spend per creative?

Below roughly £20 a creative has not had enough delivery for its numbers to mean anything, so a comparison would be noise rather than a result. The threshold protects both of us from drawing conclusions from too little data. Note this applies to each creative you include in a claim, not to all 40 at once.

How quickly will I receive everything?

Target delivery is within 48 hours after a complete brief and usable assets have been received.

Can I request revisions?

One correction round is included for factual inaccuracies or branding errors. Complete changes of direction and additional concepts are separately chargeable.

Can I use the ads commercially?

Yes, final delivered assets may be used to advertise your own product or brand.
