Working together

Your questions. Straight answers.

Direct access, clear expectations and a plan that keeps improving.

Will I work directly with you?

Yes. You work directly with me, James Parry. I stay involved in the strategy, execution and decisions behind your growth. You can talk to the person doing the work and understand what is changing, why it matters and what comes next.

Is the work outsourced or passed to an account manager?

No. I do not sell you a service and then hand your account to an outsourced agency or an account manager. I lead and deliver the work we agree together. If a project needs a specialist contribution, I will discuss it with you first and remain your direct point of contact.

Can I contact you on WhatsApp?

Yes. You have direct WhatsApp access for questions, feedback, updates and quick decisions. It keeps us close to what is happening in your business. We will agree how we communicate and what needs a scheduled call during onboarding.

What can you handle for my business?

Depending on the scope we agree, I can connect Google and Meta ads, creative production, landing pages and websites, CRM, follow-up automation and profit reporting. We start with the areas that will make the biggest difference to your business and build from there.

How do we get started?

Book a growth call below. Bring your website, the channels you use, your current or planned ad budget and the main problem you want to solve. We will discuss the opportunity, what I would prioritise and whether working together is a good fit.

What happens after the growth call?

If there is a fit, we agree the scope, fees, priorities and next steps before work starts. Onboarding covers the account access, assets, tracking and business information needed to get moving. You will know what is included and what I need from you.

Is this a one-off setup or ongoing optimisation?

Ongoing work is built around continuous optimisation and improvement. I review the data, identify bottlenecks, test creative and landing pages, improve follow-up and adjust campaigns as we learn. Launch is the starting point; the next decision comes from what the results show.

How do you decide what to improve next?

I look across the whole customer journey: the advert, the click, the page, the enquiry or purchase, the follow-up and the margin left over. We prioritise the constraint holding growth back, then test a change and use the evidence to decide what to do next.

How will I know what is working?

You get clear visibility into the work and performance. Where included in your setup, live reporting brings together spend, revenue, costs and profit so we can make decisions using your business economics. We agree the reporting and review rhythm during onboarding.

Do you look beyond ROAS and lead volume?

Yes. A strong platform metric only matters if it supports the business. For ecommerce, we look at product costs, margin and acquisition costs. For lead generation, we look at lead quality, follow-up and the path to a customer, using the data available from your systems.

Is the software really included at no extra cost?

Access to the growth software shown above is included at no extra software access cost when I deploy it in your business. Usage credits and metered services, such as AI generation, messaging or calls, are separate. We agree what you need and any additional usage costs before setup.

Are ad spend and third-party costs included?

Ad spend is separate from my fee. Any usage charges, third-party subscriptions or additional production requirements are discussed as part of the scope so you can understand the full budget before we begin.

Do I need to replace my existing website or CRM?

Not automatically. I review what you already have and work out what can be improved. If your current tools do the job, we can work with them. A replacement should solve a real problem, and any rebuild or migration is agreed before it happens.

What do you need from me?

Access to the relevant accounts, your brand assets, information about your offer and customers, and a clear view of the numbers behind your business. Timely feedback and approvals help us keep moving. I will make the requirements clear during onboarding.

Who owns the advertising accounts and business assets?

Your business should retain ownership of its advertising accounts and business data, with access granted so I can do the work. We clarify ownership, permissions and handover for any new assets in the agreed scope.

How quickly will I see results?

That depends on your starting point, offer, budget, tracking and sales cycle. Some improvements can be made quickly; others need enough data and testing time to assess properly. We agree sensible milestones for your business rather than promising a universal timeline.

Can you guarantee the same results as these case studies?

No. These examples show work and outcomes from particular businesses and reporting periods. Your results will depend on your market, offer, budget and execution. I focus on clear measurement, disciplined testing and continuous improvement.

What does it cost, and is there a minimum commitment?

The fee and commitment depend on the work your business needs. We discuss those on the growth call and confirm the scope, payment terms and any notice period before you decide to go ahead.

Can you support both ecommerce and lead generation?

Yes. The examples here cover both. Ecommerce work connects acquisition with conversion and margin; lead-generation work connects campaigns with qualification, CRM and follow-up. The setup is shaped around how your business turns interest into revenue.

What if priorities change as we work together?

We talk directly, review the evidence and adjust the plan. Continuous improvement includes challenging the original assumptions. If a change affects the agreed scope or budget, we agree it together before adding the work.
